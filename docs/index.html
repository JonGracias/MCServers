Download the installer

Download it, then double-click the file. It installs Prism Launcher if you do not have it, sets up the modpack, and points it at the server. Nothing else to configure.

Windows will warn you. It does this for every downloaded installer that is not code-signed. Your browser may say the file is unusual - choose Keep. Windows may then show "Windows protected your PC" - choose More info, then Run anyway.

When it finishes, open Prism Launcher, sign in to your Microsoft account, and press Play on Normal Survival. The first launch downloads the mods and connects you automatically. Every later launch re-checks the pack, so you always have the same mods as everyone else.

Rather not download anything? Use one line instead.

Press Windows + X, choose Terminal, paste this and press Enter. It does exactly the same thing, and because nothing is saved to disk there is no warning to click through.
